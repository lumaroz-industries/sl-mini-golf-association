A **20-member Indian team** is representing the country at the Minigolf Open Asian Championships 2026 in **Pran Buri, Thailand**, competing in team, individual, youth and mixed-pair events.

The squad is led by Dr Surajsingh Yeotikar, with coaches Ashish Bhakare and Yajvendar Singh Yadav. Maharashtra's **Saurabh Pise** and **Suyash Lahane** are part of the team. Best wishes, Team India!
