Twenty players. One flag. One mission. India sent a 20-member contingent to Pran Buri, Thailand, for the Minigolf Open Asian Championships 2026 — and every one of them stepped onto the course carrying the country's name.

## A full squad on the Asian stage

The Indian team competed across every format on offer: **team**, **individual**, **youth** and **mixed-pair** events. That spread matters — it shows a squad deep enough to field players at every level, from the youngest competitors to the most experienced.

## The people behind the team

The contingent travelled under the supervision of **Dr Surajsingh Yeotikar**, with **Ashish Bhakare** and **Yajvendar Singh Yadav** as coaches — guiding the players through every round, every lane and every pressure putt.

## Familiar names from Maharashtra's Top 7

Two names on that list will be familiar to anyone who follows this site: **Saurabh Pise** and **Suyash Lahane**, both part of [Maharashtra's Top 7](/players). Seeing them on the Asian stage is exactly the journey this community exists to celebrate.

## Why it matters

Every international appearance grows Indian mini golf — more players inspired, more clubs forming, more eyes on the sport. Twenty players on one Asian stage is a statement of how far the game has come in India.
